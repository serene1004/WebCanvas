## Design Context

### Users

Portfolio visitors and casual image editors use this public site in a browser. Their job is to open an image, make a quick visual adjustment or mark-up, and export the result without an account or server.

### Brand Personality

Clear, creative, approachable. The interface should feel like a compact professional tool, not an enterprise operations console.

### Aesthetic Direction

Light, simple Figma-inspired image workspace: cool neutral backgrounds, white tool surfaces, a measured indigo accent, and a responsive layout. Avoid the prior dashboard/admin language, excessive cards, glass effects, and neon gradients.

### Design Principles

- Put the image and its edits at the center; controls stay secondary and close at hand.
- Make every change local, reversible, and understandable without a manual.
- Use progressive disclosure: upload first, then reveal editing tools.
- Keep tool actions keyboard-accessible, touch-friendly, and readable at compact widths.
- Prefer native browser capabilities and a small dependency surface.
- Keep originals, edit history, and project files entirely in the browser.
